        Pricing & Credits

          Our pricing is simple and transparent with no subscriptions. For our initial launch, we are offering the Single Pass.         2. What is a “credit”?

          It’s simple: 1 Credit = 1 `.brushset` file conversion. Each credit you use allows you to fully process one `.brushset` file (up to 50MB) and download all of its native resolution stamps.

        3. What is the difference between the license types?

          The primary difference is the number of `.brushset` files you can convert (one credit is used per file). However, the user experience also improves with the larger packs:

          • A Single Pass license is designed for a simple, one-file conversion. You upload your file and are taken directly to a download page for that single file.
          • The Hobbyist, Creator, and Pro Packs unlock a more powerful workflow. After converting multiple files, you are taken to a "Download Center" where you can see a list of all your converted files. From there, you can download each file individually or use the "Download All as .ZIP" button to get everything in one master file, neatly organized into folders for you.
        4. Do my credits expire?

          Never. Your credits are yours until you use them.

        Technical Questions

        1. What brush types are supported?

          Our service is designed exclusively for Procreate stamp brushes. It is not designed for dynamic painting, texture, or pattern brushes. Our tool extracts the core stamp image (the 'shape source') at its native resolution. For best results, the original stamp image should be 1024x1024px or larger.

        2. What is the maximum file size?

          Each `.brushset` file can be up to 50MB. For sets larger than this, we recommend splitting them into smaller sets within Procreate before uploading to ensure a fast and reliable conversion.

        3. Are the converted PNG files transparent?

          Currently, all converted PNGs are exported with a solid white background to ensure maximum compatibility and processing speed. The white background can be easily removed in any standard design software.

        4. Do I need an iPad or Mac to use this service?

          Not at all! Our converter is a web-based tool that works in any modern browser on any device, including Windows, Mac, Android, and iOS.

        5. Can I convert brushes from Procreate Dreams?

          Our converter is compatible with standard `.brushset` files from Procreate 5 and newer. At this time, it does not support files from Procreate Dreams or very old, legacy Procreate versions due to different file structures.

        File & Download Questions

        1. How do I find my `.brushset` files on my iPad?

          You first need to export your brushes from Procreate to the Files app.

          • In Procreate: Open the Brush Library, swipe left on the brush set, and tap “Share”.
          • Save to Files: In the share menu, choose “Save to Files”. We recommend creating a folder named “Brush Exports” to keep things organized.
        2. How do I download my files after conversion?

          It depends on your license type:

          • Single-Credit Users: You will be taken to a page with a single "Download File" button.
          • Multi-Credit Users: You will be taken to a "Download Session" page. Here, you can download each converted file individually or use the "Download All as .ZIP" button to get everything in one master file. The downloaded batch is neatly organized into folders for you.
        3. How long are my download links valid?

          For your security, each converted file is available to download for 60 minutes from the moment its conversion is complete. After this time, the download link expires and the file is permanently deleted from our servers.

          If you need to access your active downloads again, simply re-enter your license key on the main page. This will give you access to your Download Center, where you can see a full history of your conversions and download any files that are still within their 60-minute window.

        4. Where do my downloaded files go?

          After conversion, your `.zip` file is downloaded directly through your browser. You can find it in your browser’s “Downloads” list or in your computer’s “Downloads” folder. On mobile, check your “Files” app.

        Trust & Safety

        1. Is it safe? Do you keep my brushes?

          Your privacy and intellectual property are our top priorities. We do not keep your files. When you upload a `.brushset` file, it is handled in a secure, temporary space for processing and then automatically cleared once the conversion is complete. Files exist only as long as needed for the task and are never stored.
